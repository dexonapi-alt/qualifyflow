# ADR-0023 — Centered driver profile flight, without fading

2026-11-19. Owner clarifies that local hover scaling is insufficient: the driver card should move into the middle using Framer Motion-style animation. Owner explicitly rejects fading the content. This supersedes ADR0022's local driver-card zoom/blur behavior; the restrained feedback on other interfaces remains.

Use Motion for React14.14.0, installed with an exact version. Animate only translation and scale from the source rectangle to the viewport center, then back on dismissal. Entry is340ms, return240ms, with controlled easing and no spring. No opacity, content dimming or crossfade is used. Background components remain mounted at full opacity. Transparent native-dialog backdrop animates blur only; a1.5px blur preserves the visible page context. Source space remains reserved while its card is represented in the centered view.

Native modal semantics keep background actions inert during focused viewing, with accessible labeling, explicit close, Escape, backdrop dismissal, focus wrapping/restoration and body-scroll cleanup. Opening from hover latches until dismissal, avoiding pointer-leave flicker as the card moves away. A visible Expand button supports keyboard and touch. Reduced motion uses immediate placement without spatial travel or animated blur. Existing file/engagement actions still invoke the real authorized routes and can navigate immediately.

No backend/API, authentication, membership or state-machine change. Motion adds bundle cost; the current build passes but reports a JavaScript chunk-size warning (roughly521kB minified/157kB gzip). Lazy-loading the animation layer is a future optimization, not a reason to hide the warning or replace the actual interaction with a mockup. Reference: https://motion.dev/docs/react-layout-animations.
