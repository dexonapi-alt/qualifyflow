# ADR-0026 — Personal driver onboarding, separate from company authority

2026-11-19. The owner explicitly authorizes a real driver experience after the read-only product audit established that driver identities are records, not login accounts. This extends the optional future driver journey in Documents 01/03 without altering those specifications. Preserve the managed-service model, employer decisions and existing security controls.

The driver workspace has no administrative sidebar. My onboarding presents the invited company engagement, explicitly shared document requests, own submissions, processing state and real accepted-evidence progress. It does not expose rosters, company settings, internal reports/findings, arbitrary driver records or specialist controls. Multiple independently granted engagements can be selected without merging driver identities across tenants. Company/staff assistance remains supported.

`DriverPortalAccess` binds the existing opaque, expiring, single-use invitation to exact tenant/driver/engagement IDs. Acceptance creates or authenticates a User, then activates the personal grant, never a company Membership. Existing accounts must authenticate as the invited email. Existing MFA/session/CSRF/origin enforcement remains; a mismatched signed-in account must sign out. No name/email-only driver matching. Whole-company user-management authority plus actual driver access is required to invite/revoke. Transfers, ended/suspended engagements, tenant restrictions and revocation fail closed. Re-invitation creates a new traceable grant; it does not overwrite access history.

Assignments remain immutable. Driver-facing instructions/visibility are separate append-only encrypted DriverDocumentRequest revisions, optimistic version checked under the tenant lock. The database rejects revision updates/deletes. Sharing is explicit and restricted to authorized company administrators/reviewers/specialists. Generic company invitation screens do not manage personal grants.

Personal submissions reuse the exact validated, encrypted, idempotent, quota-bound evidence receipt implementation. `submitted_via` persists staff versus driver provenance. The scan worker checks the corresponding current authorization before and after the real antivirus operation. Staff retain the existing review endpoint and authority. Drivers cannot assign requirements, review their own evidence or attach intake sources. Downloads require own authorship, a current personal grant, a still-shared request, scan clearance and integrity verification. Staff filenames and internal findings are omitted from personal responses. Progress means accepted evidence against shared requirements, never full onboarding/legal qualification.

Migrations: nov19001 personal grants; nov19002 immutable request revisions; nov19003 evidence origin. The modular FastAPI monolith, separate worker, PostgreSQL/Alembic and React architecture are retained. Delivery remains local/synthetic only. The existing outbox delivers invitations to the local mail sink; no real outbound notification provider or new external integration is enabled.

Verification and exact limits: docs/design/DRIVER_WORKSPACE.md; backend/tests/test_driver_portal.py; frontend/scripts/driver-portal-review.mjs. Other milestone exits and the specialist training exercise remain open.
